## GAP PRIORITY MATRIX

### P0 — Critical for Daily Operations (Block testing)
1. **Ownership Transfer Workflow** — Every management company does these daily
2. **Statement & Coupon Generation** — Required for monthly billing
3. **Fund Accounting** — Required for reserve fund separation
4. **Credit Memos** — Required for AP corrections
5. **Lockbox Processing** — Major payment intake channel

### P1 — Required for Production Launch
6. **Association Onboarding Wizard** — Structured new-community setup
7. **New Owner Setup Action Items** — Template-driven onboarding
8. **Print Queue** — Physical mail still required for FL HOA notices
9. **1098 Tax Form Generation** — Annual requirement
10. **Mobile Homeowner App** — Capacitor is configured but needs UI

### P2 — Competitive Advantage Features
11. **Bankruptcy Workflow** — Legal compliance
12. **Bank Returns/Transition** — Banking operations
13. **Service Contract Auto-AP** — Automate recurring payables
14. **Amenity Booking System** — High demand from communities
15. **Package Management** — Condo/gated community feature
16. **Bulk Action Item Stepping** — Efficiency feature
17. **Decision-Required Action Items** — Workflow branching

### P3 — Nice to Have / Future
18. Visitor Parking Management
19. Security Patrol System
20. Key Tracking / Incident Reports
21. Short-term Rental Management
22. Discussion Forum / Classifieds
