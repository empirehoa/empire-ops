## Business Model

FixIQ operates as a **captive maintenance provider** for EMG communities:

- Work orders flow directly from Vera's work order system
- Pricing is pre-negotiated for common task types
- Billing integrated through Vera's AP module
- CAMs can dispatch FixIQ with one click

> [!tip] FixIQ's competitive advantage is **zero acquisition cost** — 100% of leads come from EMG's work order pipeline. The entire sales process is eliminated.

## Vera Platform Integration

- **Work Order Routing** — EMG work orders auto-route to FixIQ for eligible task types
- **Vendor Portal** — Technicians update job status, upload photos, log time
- **Recurring Work Orders** — Vera's [[Automation Agents|automation engine]] generates scheduled maintenance
- **Invoicing** — FixIQ invoices flow into EMG's AP approval workflow
- **GPS/Geolocation** — Technician location tracking via Capacitor mobile app
