# Empire Management Group

> [!info] Empire Management Group is Florida's community association management company, managing 255 communities with 28,391 doors across 9 offices. It is the primary revenue driver for [[Riance LLC Overview|Riance LLC]].

## Service Offerings

### Core Services
- **Financial Management** — Assessment billing, collections, AP/AR, GL accounting, budget preparation, reserve studies
- **Administrative Management** — Board meeting support, elections, record keeping, compliance tracking
- **Maintenance Management** — Work orders, vendor management, inspections, preventive maintenance
- **Communications** — Homeowner portal, email blasts, text notifications, call center
- **Compliance & Legal** — Violation enforcement, collections escalation, Florida statute compliance
- **Insurance Management** — COI tracking, claims coordination, master policy administration

### Value-Add Services
- **AI-Powered Analytics** — Financial anomaly detection, predictive delinquency, board meeting packet generation
- **Vendor Network** — Preferred vendor program, bid management, 3-way matching
- **Technology Platform** — [[Architecture Overview|Vera]] homeowner/board portals, mobile app (Capacitor)
- **Cross-Company Services** — [[Wind Fire Water|Restoration]], [[FixIQ|Maintenance]], [[Riance Realty|Real Estate]] via sister companies

## Competitive Position

> [!info] Empire competes with both national players (FirstService, Associa) and regional firms. See [[Competitive Landscape]] for full analysis.

**Strengths:**
- Local Florida expertise with deep statutory knowledge (Ch. 718/719/720)
- AI-powered platform ([[Architecture Overview|Vera]]) as differentiator
- Integrated ecosystem with sister companies
- Strong Central FL market position

**Challenges:**
- Geographic concentration in Central FL
- Revenue concentration risk (see [[Growth Opportunities]])
- Talent acquisition for CAM positions
- Competing against well-funded national players
