## Florida Market Advantage

> [!tip] Florida experiences an average of 1-2 hurricanes per year and frequent tropical storms. HOA communities with aging buildings are especially vulnerable. WFW has a captive market of 255 communities through EMG.

**Key factors:**
- Florida's hurricane season (June-November) drives consistent demand
- SB 4D milestone inspection requirements reveal structural issues needing remediation
- Aging condo buildings (especially coastal) need ongoing restoration work
- Insurance claim complexity creates demand for managed restoration services
