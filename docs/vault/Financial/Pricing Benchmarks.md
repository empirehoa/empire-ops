## Value Justification for Price Increase

### What Boards Get with Vera (vs. competitors)

| Feature | Empire/Vera | Typical Competitor | Value |
|---------|------------|-------------------|-------|
| AI meeting packet generation | Included | Manual / $500+ per meeting | $6K/year saved |
| Automated violation lifecycle | Included | Manual CAM time | 4-8 hours/week saved |
| Online payment portal | Included | $2-5/door extra | $3.50/door saved |
| Financial intelligence alerts | Included | Not available | Risk reduction |
| Mobile app (iOS + Android) | Included | Web-only or $1-3/door | $2/door saved |
| Integrated restoration/maintenance | Via WFW/FixIQ | Third-party coordination | Faster response |
| Predictive delinquency | AI agent | Not available | Revenue protection |

### Estimated Total Value Delivered

```
Portal: $3.50/door + Mobile: $2/door + AI features: ~$5/door equivalent
= ~$10.50/door in additional value vs. basic competitor
```

> [!tip] A $3/door increase still leaves Empire delivering ~$7.50/door more value than competitors charge for. The pricing headroom is significant.
