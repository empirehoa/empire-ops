# Competitive Landscape

> [!info] [[Empire Management Group]] competes against both national conglomerates and regional Florida management companies. This document maps the competitive landscape and identifies differentiation opportunities.

---

## Competitive Tiers

### National Players

| Company | Scale | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---------|-------|-----------|------------|
| **FirstService Residential** | 9,000+ communities | Largest in North America; brand recognition; deep resources | Impersonal; high turnover; slow technology adoption |
| **Associa** | 7,000+ communities | National scale; diverse services | Bureaucratic; inconsistent service quality |
| **RealManage** | 2,000+ communities | Technology focus; good portals | Smaller scale; limited brand recognition |

### Technology Platforms (EMG competitors for tech-savvy boards)

| Platform | Focus | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| **CINC Systems** | Software for management cos | Good financial engine; reporting | No management services; dated UI |
| **AppFolio** | Property management software | Modern UI; broad feature set | Not HOA-specific; weak on compliance |
| **Buildium** | Small management companies | Easy to use; affordable | Limited HOA features; no FL compliance |
| **Vantaca** | HOA management software | Good workflow engine; FL-aware | Being replaced by Vera; aging platform |
| **FrontSteps** | Community portals | Good homeowner portal | Limited management features |
| **CondoControl** | Condo management | Simple interface | Weak accounting; no FL compliance |

### Regional Florida Competitors

| Company | Markets | Size | Notes |
|---------|---------|------|-------|
| **Castle Group** | SE Florida | 500+ communities | Strong in South FL; recently acquired |
| **Leland Management** | Central FL | 200+ communities | Direct competitor in core market |
| **Greenacre Properties** | Central FL | 150+ communities | Price competitor |
| **Sentry Management** | Multi-state (FL focus) | 1,500+ communities | Large regional player |

## Competitive Advantages (Empire / Vera)

### Technology Moat

| Capability | Empire/Vera | Typical Competitor |
|-----------|------------|-------------------|
| AI-powered operations (VeRAI) | 15+ AI features live | None or basic |
| Statutory compliance engine | Hard-coded FL deadlines | Manual tracking |
| Automated violation lifecycle | Full auto-escalation | Manual or partial |
| Real-time financial analytics | Live dashboards | Monthly reports |
| Predictive intelligence | 5 AI agents (financial health, retention, etc.) | None |
| Integrated ecosystem | 4 companies, one platform | Single company |
| Mobile app | Capacitor iOS + Android | Web-only or basic |

### Service Moat

| Factor | Empire | National Players | Regional Players |
|--------|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| CAM-to-community ratio | ~10:1 | 15-20:1 | 10-15:1 |
| Technology platform | Proprietary (Vera) | Off-the-shelf | Off-the-shelf |
| Response time | Same-day target | 24-48 hours | Same-day |
| Board communication | AI-generated packets | Manual | Manual |
| Cross-service (restoration, realty, maintenance) | Integrated | None | Limited |

## Pricing Comparison

| Service | Empire | National (Avg) | Regional (Avg) |
|---------|--------|---------------|----------------|
| Per-door fee (medium community) | ~$22 | $25-30 | $20-25 |
| Setup fee | $2-5K | $5-10K | $1-3K |
| After-hours premium | Varies | $150-300/call | Varies |
| Estoppel fee | $250 | $250-500 | $200-350 |
| Technology fee | $0 (bundled) | $2-5/door | $0-3/door |

> [!tip] Empire is currently pricing **below market** for most tiers. The [[Architecture Overview|Vera platform]] justifies premium pricing — boards get more for the same or slightly higher fee. See [[Pricing Benchmarks]] for optimization analysis.

## Threat Analysis

### Threats to Monitor

| Threat | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|------------|
| National player acquires regional FL competitor | High | Medium | Build sticky technology moat; long-term contracts |
| New tech startup targets FL HOA market | Medium | Medium | Move fast; expand feature gap |
| Board self-management trend (DIY platforms) | Low | Low | Only viable for small communities; Empire targets 50+ doors |
| Economic downturn reduces management budgets | Medium | Medium | Demonstrate ROI; tiered pricing |
| Regulation changes (de-regulation) | Low | Very Low | Statutory compliance remains complex regardless |

### Opportunities from Competition

| Opportunity | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Competitor dissatisfaction** | CAM turnover at national firms creates switching opportunities |
| **Technology gap** | Boards frustrated with outdated platforms seek modern solutions |
| **SB 4D compliance** | Firms overwhelmed by inspection requirements create openings |
| **Acquisition targets** | Small firms with 50-100 communities are acquisition candidates |
| **White-label** | Regional firms want technology but can't build it ([[New Revenue Streams|Stream #1]]) |
